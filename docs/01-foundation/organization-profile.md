## What is Blue Arbor ##

Blue Arbor is a fictional SaaS company with around 2500 employees and 15,000 users. Oblivara provides membership-based businesses with tools to manage subscription billing, customer accounts, analytics and support tools. Blue Arbor mainly works with gyms, online content creators, and other companies that offer memberships. It is located in California and provides support to users all over America along with businesses in the EU. Among the many services Blue Arbor(BA) provides, BA also offers software support for processing payments. BA uses NIST CSF 2.0 to organize its cyber-risk program and it also must comply with GDPR, PCI DSS, and CCPA.

## Services
Subscription billing - processes recurring payments, renewals, refunds, and invoices. Manages card data, Payment tokens, billing address, transaction history. personal data(GDPR/CCPA) card data(PCI DSS)
Member portal - lets users manage accounts, subscriptions, and privacy preferences. Portal holds personal including name, email, password hash, MFA, etc.
Merchant dashboard - allows businesses to manage customers, transactions, permissions, and reports. 
Customer analytics - shows retention, purchases, engagement, and trends. Creates data transparency questions for GDPR, and sale-or-sharing/opt-out questions for CCPA
Marketing automation - renewal reminders, offers, checkout messages. 
support and refund portal - Handles account problems, refund, disputes, and attachments. 
Integration marketplace - connects platform to payment processors, CRMs, email providers, and analytic vendors. Shares account, transaction, tokens, and API activity to third party analytic and payment vendors creating third party risk.
