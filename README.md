# Privacy GRC Homelab

I am building this project to learn how privacy, governance, risk, compliance, and security work together in an enterprise environment. The technical environment is my homelab, while the business scenario is a fictional company called **Blue Arbor Technologies**.

My goal is to practice the work performed in privacy, GRC, IT audit, and technology-risk roles—not just collect finished templates.

## What I am building

Over time, this repository will contain:

- An organizational profile and privacy program scope
- System and personal-data inventories
- Data-flow diagrams and a record of processing activities
- Privacy impact assessments and a risk register
- Policies, procedures, and a control catalog
- Vendor and incident-response assessments
- Control tests, findings, and remediation plans
- Privacy metrics and executive reports

## How I will document my work

Each artifact should explain:

1. What I decided
2. Why I made that decision
3. What evidence or assumptions I used
4. How the decision affects privacy or security risk
5. What I would verify in a real organization

## Repository structure

```text
docs/
  01-foundation/       Program context, scope, and governance
  02-inventory/        Systems, data, vendors, and data flows
  03-requirements/     Framework and requirement mappings
  04-risk/             Risk assessments and privacy impact assessments
  05-controls/         Policies, controls, and implementation evidence
  06-operations/       Privacy requests, incidents, and procedures
  07-assurance/        Testing, findings, and remediation
  08-reporting/        Metrics and executive reporting
notes/                 Decision log and learning notes
```

## Safety rules

- Use only fictional organizations, users, and data.
- Do not upload University of Michigan tickets, logs, credentials, configurations, or nonpublic information.
- Do not upload passwords, API keys, private keys, or raw sensitive evidence.
- Clearly separate facts, assumptions, and recommendations.
- Do not claim that this project certifies legal or regulatory compliance.
